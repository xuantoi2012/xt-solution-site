# Third-party design assets

- Be Vietnam Pro, @fontsource/be-vietnam-pro 5.3.0. Local WOFF2 normal weights 400, 500, 600, 700 with Latin, Latin Extended and Vietnamese subsets. Source: https://fontsource.org/fonts/be-vietnam-pro and official npm package. License: licenses/Be-Vietnam-Pro-OFL.txt (SIL Open Font License).
- Lucide, lucide-static 1.55.0. Selected official SVG icons combined into icons.svg; symbol names are local aliases. Source: https://lucide.dev/ and official npm package. License and notices: licenses/Lucide-LICENSE.txt.

No third-party font or icon CDN is needed at runtime.
